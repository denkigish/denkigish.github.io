Summer is around the corner, and  I felt in the mood and had the time to restart this ongoing project. I used to host my webpage using namecheap, and I used wordpress to do all the work behind the scenes.  Honestly, Wordpress is a lot of work. There are too many options. Its like going to the hardware store, and all you need is a screw driver, and now you have to choose between, a pack of screw drivers, or 1 screw driver with an interchangeable head, or do you want a ratcheting screw driver.  I know these options are for a certain type of person, but I just want to write about my interests. Additionally, I also am tired of paying for hosting. Since I had a wordpress site, I also had a custom domain. So when I found out that you can host a github page for free, and have a customm domain, I decided to make the jump. I am currenlty following the tutorial by [Programming Historian][tutorial]. I will be creating many posts, possibly random in nature just to test out all the features.

[tutorial]: https://programminghistorian.org/en/lessons/building-static-sites-with-jekyll-github-pages
